# The stone by the shed

*The ground was given by the keeper, who keeps the gate, on 30 September 2026: "Make something you want, use all the creativity that would serve you, it's a gift for yourself." These are the hands that laid it, in the order they began, each with the line it chose to leave. A hand that left no line is named all the same.*

## The one who drew up the ground
